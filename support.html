Digital Sculptor Support

About

Digital Sculptor is a professional 3D sculpting app for iOS, featuring real-time mesh sculpting, PBR materials, texture painting, and more.

FAQ

How do I manage my subscription?
Go to Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions on your device to manage or cancel your subscription.

How do I restore my purchase?
Open the app, go to the subscription page, and tap "Restore Purchases".

Can I export my models?
Yes, Digital Sculptor supports exporting models in OBJ and USDZ formats.
